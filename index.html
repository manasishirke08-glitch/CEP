What is financial literacy?

Financial literacy is the ability to understand and manage money: saving, budgeting, borrowing, banking and making decisions. In rural areas, this knowledge protects hard-earned income from debt traps and fraud, and helps families use banks, schemes and digital payments with confidence.

Banking awareness

What is a bank?

A bank keeps your money safe, pays interest on savings, gives loans and lets you send or receive money.

What is a savings account, passbook and bank statement?

A savings account stores money and pays interest. A passbook is a booklet that records deposits and withdrawals. A bank statement is a detailed list of all transactions for a period.

What are an ATM and a debit card?

An ATM is a machine to withdraw cash and check balance. A debit card spends money already in your account; a credit card lets you borrow and repay later with interest.

What is a nominee?

The person you name to receive your account money if something happens to you. Always add one.

Bank account vs digital payment app

A bank account holds your money. A payment app (like a UPI app) is only a tool that moves money from your bank account to another.

How to open a bank account

  1. Choose a suitable bank or Post Office near you.
  2. Check the account’s eligibility rules.
  3. Ask the bank which documents are needed.
  4. Fill in and submit the application.
  5. Complete the bank’s verification.
  6. Keep your account details, PIN and passbook secure.

Requirements vary by bank and account type. Banking services for villages include branches, bank mitras (banking correspondents), post offices, ATMs and mobile banking.

Digital banking and online safety

Know the basics

  • UPI: instant bank-to-bank payments using a mobile app.
  • QR payment: scan a shop’s QR code, check the name, enter your PIN to pay.
  • Phishing: fake messages or calls that trick you into sharing secrets.
  • Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN or CVV with anyone, including “bank staff”. You never need a PIN to receive money.
